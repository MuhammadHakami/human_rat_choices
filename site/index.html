Same states,
different choices

Humans and rats solved the same mazes. Agents trained on human choices loop less and find goals in rooms they never saw. On top of them, a mushroom-body circuit, modelled on the insect learning centre, learns new goals with local plasticity only, better than backpropagation-trained PPO.

Muhammad Y. Hakami

Asked in review

What the chairs and four reviewers asked, what changed, and where to see it live on this page. Every question, with pointers, is answered in supplement Sec. 1; the main claims are unchanged.

Is the gain only frozen and only for the trained goal?Chairs, FtUu, 6g1M, GQMPYes, and the claim is scoped to that. A registered moved-goal test shows frozen agents falling below uniform, so learners were added: PPO, local three-factor rules, the mushroom-body circuit and the hippocampal goal map.Play: "Goal moved" scenarios →
Does the pose belief leak the layout or the goal?FtUuNo. A Bayes filter over an open-arena template atlas: no wall map, goal or position. The input block lists all 252 numbers the agent gets.Inside the model: Input →
Sampled or greedy actions? How many failures are loops?FtUuSampled, as registered; greedy lowers every arm, and a Lean proof shows why: a deterministic memoryless agent that repeats a state loops forever. About 95% of failures are loops.Play: Actions = greedy · Proved in Lean →
Is the human gain just less looping?FtUuMostly, and that is the finding: humans rarely step back onto a visited cell (3.8% of moves; rats 14.4%), the agents inherit it (−8.2 pp revisits), and at equal trapping the gain shrinks to 1.7 pp.Humans vs rats →
Is the rat null just unpredictable labels?FtUuRat choices are learnable (56% held-out accuracy vs 32% for legal labels) but carry about half the predictable information of human choices; the null is stated for these reconstructed inputs only.Evidence: transfer →
Specific to PPO? Does the prior help when learning continues?UYhB, FtUuTested with PPO and local rules without backpropagation: the prior never speeds up new-goal learning. A brain-constrained circuit with three-factor plasticity and awake replay learns the new goal +12.4 pp better than PPO.Learn & forget →
Neural results out of nowhere?UYhB, GQMPNow one question inside the story: is the content in the view or in the choice? Predicted mouse cortex and Rosetta geometry in the main text; recorded rat and human brains, replayed through the same networks on the same states, here and in the supplement.Evidence: recorded brains →
Unequal text and image information? Do caption words leak the action?6g1M, GQMPReplaced by a matched-content study in which image, caption and action describe the same moment; captions without any action word predict the move equally well (≤1 pp change).Evidence: vision–language–action →
Weak absolute performance, small memoryless policy?GQMPStated in the limitations and now measured on the animals' own layouts: humans 97%, rats 85%, frozen agents 55–57%, the goal map 66–70%.Evidence: the animals' own mazes →
Post-hoc follow-ups; can the pre-registration be verified?GQMPEach follow-up is marked. Every study ships a PROTOCOL.json written before its outcome, with the hash of the exact script; amendments and any outcome seen early are disclosed in ADDENDUM files.data/audit/v4n_* on GitHub →
Equivalence margins, p-values, seeds are not animalsGQMPEquivalence language dropped; 95% CIs everywhere, Holm only within registered families; seeds measure training variability, not biological replication.Evidence →
Dense text, mixed units, title; side studiesChairs, UYhBMain text halved, percentage points throughout, retitled to the supported claim; replay, cortex and alignment studies moved to the supplement.Supplement →
